Why WriteIt Exists

Designed by a Health and Social Care Assessor and IQA with years of frontline experience, WriteIt was built to close a very real gap: care workers who are exceptional at their jobs but struggle to transfer that knowledge onto paper. The demands of care leave little time for academic writing — this tool bridges that gap.

What the Tool Does

  • Writes assignments in first person, linking your real practice to theory
  • Uses UK case studies and legislation (Care Act 2014, Children Act 1989, MCA 2005, etc.)
  • Generates fully in-text referenced work in your chosen style
  • Provides a complete reference list with live UK web links
  • Uses natural human language to minimise AI detection flags
  • Personalises every answer using your scenario and workplace setting

Who This Is For

  • Support Workers completing NVQs and Diplomas
  • Care Providers needing staff certificated for OFSTED and CQC inspections
  • Employers wanting staff to progress qualifications quickly
  • Assessors and IQAs who want a study guide resource for learners

Security & Privacy

  • No scenario data is stored or shared with third parties
  • All API communications are encrypted (HTTPS)
  • GDPR compliant data handling
  • Rate limiting and abuse protection active at all times
